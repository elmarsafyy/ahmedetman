TITLE:
What If the Moon Crashed Into Earth? 🌕💥 #Shorts

DESCRIPTION:
What would REALLY happen if the Moon slammed into Earth? Spoiler: it wouldn't even make it here in one piece. 🌕💥

Right now, the Moon sits about 239,000 miles away. So let's pull it closer and see what happens.

🌊 At half the distance, the Moon looks twice as big in the night sky, and its pull on our oceans gets 8x stronger. High tide wouldn't just roll in. It would swallow entire coastlines. New York, Miami, Boston, Charleston... underwater twice a day.

🌋 Pull it closer, and the Moon starts tugging on the ground itself. Earthquakes across the country. Yellowstone, Mount St. Helens and volcanoes all over the Ring of Fire waking up at once.

💥 Then, about 11,000 miles out, things get wild. Earth pulls so much harder on the Moon's near side than its far side that it rips the Moon apart. Scientists call that the Roche limit.

🪐 The pieces would spread into a giant ring around Earth, just like Saturn's. Imagine looking up from your backyard and seeing that. Beautiful... for a little while.

🔥 Because then the debris starts falling. Massive chunks raining down every single day. The sky glowing red-hot. Oceans boiling away. The crust melting into a sea of magma. Game over.

🤯 Here's the crazy part: it's already happened. About 4.5 billion years ago, a Mars-sized planet called Theia smashed into the young Earth, and the debris from that crash became the Moon.

So the Moon was literally born from a collision. Let's just hope it never comes home.

⚡ Quick facts
• The Moon is about 239,000 miles from Earth.
• Tidal force grows with the cube of closeness: half the distance = 8x the pull.
• The Moon's Roche limit is about 11,000 miles from Earth's center.
• The giant-impact (Theia) theory is NASA's leading explanation for where the Moon came from.
• Relax: the Moon is actually drifting AWAY from us, about 1.5 inches every year.

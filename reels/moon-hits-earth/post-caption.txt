What if the Moon crashed into Earth? 🌕💥🌍

It wouldn't hit us in one piece. Around 11,000 miles out, Earth's gravity would tear it apart (the Roche limit). We'd get a ring like Saturn's for a while, then a rain of debris that would melt the planet's crust.

The wild part: something like this already happened. 4.5 billion years ago a Mars-sized planet called Theia slammed into the young Earth, and the debris became the Moon.
